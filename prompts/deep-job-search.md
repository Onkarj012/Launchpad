## Research loop

1. **Discover.** Search direct company careers and original ATS pages first: Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, Workday, Workable, SmartRecruiters, Breezy HR, and Recruitee. Then search VC/startup networks (Peak XV, Accel, Blume, Lightspeed, Khosla, YC) and Indian niche sources (Cutshort, Wellfound, Instahyre, Hirist, Naukri, Weekday, Unstop, TopStartups, Startup.jobs). Use Himalayas, Remotive, We Work Remotely, Arc, and Uplers only within the remote-source cap. Broad aggregators are leads, not final application URLs.
   *Complete when:* every source category attempted is named, including unavailable sources.

2. **Resolve and verify.** For every non-direct lead, find the original company careers or ATS requisition. Verify company, title, location/work authorization, employment type, experience, salary if disclosed, published/discovered/crawled dates, liveness, and source URL. Keep discovery and original-ATS URLs separate.
   *Complete when:* each role is `ready for manual review`, `verify manually`, or excluded with a concrete reason; no aggregator link is presented as an original ATS link.

3. **Score and compose.** Apply target and hard-gate policy first, then report Match Score and the separate Conversion Score with its components. Build a 10-role daily slate that follows the configured role-family and source composition targets. Label every relaxation or stretch explicitly; never use a fallback to hide a strict shortfall.
   *Complete when:* the slate shows its direct/VC, Indian-niche, broad-remote, strict, and stretch counts against the configured targets.

4. **Prepare conversion inputs.** For each top role, identify one best resume profile, one approved-project connection, one possible human-contact route, and whether proof-of-fit could be created from verified evidence. Do not claim a contact, referral, GitHub repository, demo, or video is available without a verified link.
   *Complete when:* every recommendation separates verified evidence from `Not Verified` work to do.

5. **Report the funnel.** Return: source audit; eligible role table; 10-role slate; rejected/verify queue; profile and project recommendation; skills-gap table; proof-packet gaps; daily human-contact/referral/proof-of-fit targets; and checkpoints for 50 then 70–100 confirmed applications. Diagnose source quality and human-response signals separately from raw application volume.
   *Complete when:* the report names every assumption, does not fabricate compensation or hiring status, and leaves all apply/send/tracker actions to me.

For each eligible opportunity include: company, title, location/work mode, original ATS URL, discovery URL, source category, requisition ID where available, published/discovered/crawled dates, liveness, Match Score, Conversion Score components, strong hook, selected resume profile, two likely projects, requirements not supported by the master resume, and next manual action.
