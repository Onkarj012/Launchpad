# Deep Job Search (run occasionally — heavy research)

A thorough one-shot search-and-strategy pass. Slower than the daily pull; use it weekly or when you want a full strategic sweep with ATS analysis and a 30-day plan.

To run: paste this into Cowork (the agent reads your master resume from the folder).

---

Act as my AI career coach, recruiter, ATS specialist, and sourcer. Use live web results only — never invent jobs, companies, salaries, locations, requirements, links, or hiring status. If something can't be verified, write "Not Verified".

My profile: final-year B.Tech CS (9.17 CGPA), Pune, 2026 grad, fresher. Targets: AI/LLM/Agentic/GenAI/RAG Engineer, ML Engineer, Full-Stack (Python/Next.js), SDE-1, Graduate/Associate Engineer. Locations in order: Pune, Pimpri-Chinchwad, India-remote, hybrid-Pune, international-remote. Salary ideal 8+ LPA, acceptable 6+. Treat jobs and internships equally.

Do this:
1. Analyze my resume (folder) — skills, strengths, recruiter summary (4-6 lines).
2. Match me to the top 20 role categories with a 0-100 fit score, why it fits, typical salary range, and growth potential.
3. Live search across the locations above and each role family separately. Prefer roles posted in the last 24h / 3d / 7d / 14d. Prefer direct company career pages and ATS portals over boards and agencies.
4. For each opportunity: title, company, location, remote/hybrid/onsite, posting date + freshness tier, salary (or "Not Verified"), source link, apply link, match score, missing keywords, interview likelihood.
5. Skill-gap table: skill | demand | on my resume? | priority.
6. Certifications worth doing (relevant to what you found), with provider, cost, priority.
7. ATS report: score 0-100, missing keywords, weak bullets, an ATS keyword block (only keywords true to my experience).
8. A 30-day job-search plan: daily application/outreach targets, weekly portfolio + GitHub work, recruiter/LinkedIn/referral strategy, interview prep.
9. Final shortlists: Top 10 overall, fastest interview chance, highest growth, best internships, best AI roles, best full-stack roles.

Deduplicate. Rank by match (50%), freshness (30%), location (10%), company quality (10%). Search thoroughly before ranking — don't stop after a few.

Optional: write the strongest 70+ matches into my Notion Applications database (Company, Role, Location, JD Link, Match Score, Resume Slot 3, Status = Researching).
