\begin{itemize}

  \item Designed section-aware chunking at H2/H3 wiki boundaries (not naive fixed token windows) with enriched [Series][Page][Section] metadata per chunk; per-chunk first\_appearance\_order spoiler tags assigned at ingestion time and filtered at retrieval time, ensuring users never surface lore beyond their current story progress.

  \item Designed an async ingestion pipeline (Celery workers, Redis broker, Cloudflare R2/S3 object storage) that fetches pages from the MediaWiki API, converts wikitext to structured markdown, stores raw pages in R2/S3, and persists infobox metadata in PostgreSQL.
  \item Established a \textbf{50-question per-series ground-truth eval harness} measuring retrieval MRR and end-to-end answer accuracy, gating every deploy; validated architecture against the Harry Potter wiki (~6,000 pages).
  \item Shipped two distinct consumer surfaces on a single shared retriever: a streaming chat interface with inline citations (conversational) and a hierarchically generated lore book with aggressive per-series caching (reference).
