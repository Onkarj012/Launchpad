# Source supply and employer routes

Research snapshot: 8 October 2026. Public documentation and pages only; accessed 8 October 2026 unless noted. "Verified" means a cited source explicitly documents the behavior. "Unverified" means no vendor-supported anonymous contract was found or semantics vary. This report does not claim these sources were tested in Launchpad.

Do not treat visible account APIs for Darwinbox, Zoho, BambooHR, Freshteam, iCIMS or Keka as public feeds.

## B. Company list for Pune and India-remote

Keep a company watchlist as a seed inventory, not proof that it is hiring. Record company/domain, location footprint, product/GCC/AI label, seed source/date, careers URL, ATS vendor/board identifier, last board seen, last job seen, and confidence. Recommendations: refresh company metadata quarterly, validate board existence every 1-2 weeks, and fetch live openings daily; these cadences are proposed, not vendor requirements.

* **Startup India:** government portal describes an online directory of 50K+ startups across sectors and stages. Good company discovery, no hiring proof. [Directory](https://www.startupindia.gov.in/santhali/content/sih/en/about_startup_portal.html)
* **Inc42 Datalabs:** startup/company discovery with city, sector and stage filters and refreshed data; some export/deep-profile features are paid. Funding is not hiring evidence. [Datalabs](https://inc42.com/datalabs/)
* **Tracxn:** company discovery across India and other geographies; free Lite has strict usage limits, while fuller features are paid. Useful for company seeds, not openings. [Companies overview](https://help.tracxn.com/en/articles/6635051-companies-overview), [pricing](https://tracxn.com/pricing)
* **Y Combinator:** direct jobs with India/remote filters and a 5,000+ company directory. Good startup signals; remote country restrictions still need checking. [YC jobs](https://www.ycombinator.com/jobs/role/all), [company directory](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/location/new-york/hiring)
* **VC portfolios:** official portfolio lists from Peak XV, Accel, Blume, Lightspeed and Elevation seed named employers; then find their career pages. They are not job feeds. [Peak XV](https://www.peakxv.com/companies), [Accel](https://www.accel.com/companies), [Blume](https://blume.vc/portfolio)
* **GCC/employer lists:** NASSCOM’s member directory can seed larger India/Pune employers; membership does not imply open roles. [NASSCOM directory](https://nasscom.in/members-listing/n)
* **Pune ecosystem:** Startup India and Pune incubator portfolios can seed local employers; static "top startup" articles are stale-prone. Verify company domain and career link. [Startup India](https://www.startupindia.gov.in/), [Venture Center](https://www.venturecenter.co.in/)
* **ATS slug discovery:** follow employer Careers link; inspect public sitemap; search exact company with provider domains (`boards.greenhouse.io`, `job-boards.greenhouse.io`, `jobs.lever.co`, `jobs.ashbyhq.com`, `jobs.smartrecruiters.com`, `apply.workable.com`, `*.recruitee.com`, `*.myworkdayjobs.com`). Store exact discovered URL, then validate company identity from the board. Search index hits are leads, not evidence of an active board.

The repo already has a VC query lane and named ATS boards, but search currently emphasizes a small set of providers and a limited company seed set. Add verified board URLs per employer rather than repeating broad portfolio searches.

## D. Freshness and employment

Google defines JobPosting `datePosted` as the original date the employer posted the role, `validThrough` as expiry, and `employmentType` as the employment type. For fully remote roles, applicant-country requirements should identify where candidates may work; TELECOMMUTE should not mean occasional WFH. These are schema semantics, not proof a page is correct. [Google JobPosting docs](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/structured-data/job-posting)

Parse JSON-LD fields `datePosted`, `validThrough`, `employmentType`, `jobLocation`, `jobLocationType`, `applicantLocationRequirements`, `hiringOrganization` and `url`. Ashby exposes linked data; Greenhouse exposes first_published. Google does not publish a per-ATS adoption rate, so field coverage must be measured per page. [Ashby](https://developers.ashbyhq.com/reference/jobpostinginfo), [Greenhouse](https://docs.greenhouse.io/job-board.html)

Closure evidence, strongest first: explicit ATS closed/archived status or absence from active list; detail/apply page 404/410 or explicit closed text; past validThrough; disabled/missing apply control; URL disappears from employer sitemap across refreshes. Google says expired pages should set past validThrough, return 404/410, or remove JobPosting markup; expired schema still present is a policy problem. Treat last_seen as a liveness observation, never as posted date. [Google expiry guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/structured-data/job-posting#remove-a-job-posting), [Indexing API scope](https://developers.google.com/search/apis/indexing-api/v3/quickstart)

Employment should come from explicit first-party ATS field or posting language. Leave unknown blank. "Permanent" alone does not establish full-time; remote alone does not establish India eligibility. Schema can be stale or incomplete.

+### Limits and unverified items
+
+* No source count or query budget proves a 10-role daily verified yield. Measure eligible, fresh, original-route, scored roles per request over representative days.
+* Most anonymous ATS GET rate limits are undocumented. Keep one request at a time per host, existing one-second host spacing, bounded retries, and stop on 429/403/challenge responses. Never bypass access controls.
+* ATS shape alone does not establish employer identity, early-career eligibility, pay, India remote eligibility, freshness or liveness.
+* Public Keka feed, Darwinbox anonymous endpoint, Zoho public schema, BambooHR public-list field consistency, Workday employment type, and universal portal apply URLs remain unverified.
+* No login, authenticated endpoint, paid source, crawler/job pull, credential or external write was used.
