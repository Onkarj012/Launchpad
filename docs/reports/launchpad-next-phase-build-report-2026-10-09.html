Eligibility rules

TicketWhat it does
01Pune area names (Kalyani Nagar, Hadapsar, Yerwada and others) count as Pune. "Sus" and "Maan" need Maharashtra, MH or a 411/412 pin. Chakan, Talegaon, Ranjangaon and Shikrapur are rejected. Multi-city listings with a separate Pune option pass.
02Roles carry labels beside reject reasons. A label never rejects. Night hours (IST) for remote US/EU hours and onsite night or rotating shifts. Notice risk for immediate joiners or notice of 30 days or less.
03Worldwide, anywhere and global remote roles pass when there is no country limit and no foreign work authorization. They get Remote: worldwide, no country limit.
04Lower pay floor from the spec. Undisclosed pay stays Not Verified. Internships need the stated stipend bar and a PPO path. The drop list (unpaid, equity-only, bonded and others) is enforced.
05Current employer excluded (COMPANY_EXCLUDED). Eight IT services majors pass only for AI or product engineering titles (SERVICES_MAJOR_GENERIC_ROLE).
06"Preferred" years no longer count as a hard minimum. The result flags a hard minimum of exactly 3 years.
07EOR and long-term contract classes, labelled EOR and Contract. Contracts missing a required term are dropped. Unknown terms go to verification.

master_prompt.md changed in the same commits as the rules above.
