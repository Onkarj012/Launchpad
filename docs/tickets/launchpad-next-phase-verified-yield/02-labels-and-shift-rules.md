# 02: Labels and shift rules

**What to build:** Eligibility results carry a labels list beside reasons. A label never rejects a role. Remote roles on US or EU hours get `Night hours (IST)` instead of being dropped. Onsite night or rotating shifts in Pune pass with `Night hours (IST)`. Listings that ask for immediate joiners or a notice of 30 days or less pass with `Notice risk`.
