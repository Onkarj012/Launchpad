# Human pipeline

For the five strongest daily applications, identify the most appropriate single first human: engineering recruiter or hiring manager; founder/CTO for a company of roughly 50 people or fewer; relevant engineer/alumnus only after the initial contact's silence window. Consult the contact ledger before every draft. A sent, pending, or planned contact for the same application blocks a simultaneous first message.

For about three strongest applications, identify a referral prospect with one truthful relationship signal: PCCOE alumni, Pune engineer, relevant project background, recent joiner, mutual connection, or team contributor. Apply first unless the employer explicitly requires referral submission first. Draft only on explicit request; ask whether they would be comfortable referring after reviewing the profile. Never claim a connection or referral.

Completion: every intended contact/referral has exactly one status, evidence/source link, next eligible action, and no duplicated outreach.
