# Selection

Score each factor only from evidence: responsibilities/stack 25, experience eligibility 20, India location eligibility 15, project connection 15, direct/VC source 10, human contact available 10, and freshness 5. Sum to 100. Explain zeroes and unknowns; unknown is not a pass.

75+ is `ready for manual review`; 65–74 is `manual inspect`; below 65 is usually deprioritized. No score checks `Apply?` or submits an application.

For a ten-role selection, audit: 4 AI product/applied AI; 2 full-stack/backend; 2 entry software; 1 technical solutions/implementation; 1 stretch. Require at least 4 direct/VC and 3 Indian niche; cap broad remote at 2 and stretch at 1. Report shortfalls rather than reshaping a user selection without permission.
