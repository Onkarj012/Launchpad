# Queue rules

Applications: first follow-up at three calendar days after confirmed submission; second at seven calendar days; maximum two. Cold outreach: one follow-up at four days after a confirmed send. Conversation: nudge at one day and then three days only when the ball is in the company's court. Thank-you notes follow an interview only when the user requests a draft.

No response after 14 days is `inactive for reporting`; do not send further messages without an explicit user decision. A rejection, withdrawal, or reply that closes the loop stops all scheduled nudges.
