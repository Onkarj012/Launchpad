# Artifact choices

Use the lowest-effort credible evidence: an observation from public product material; an architecture suggestion framed as a hypothesis; a link to an already-built feature/code section; or a compact approach note. Keep it to three bullets or a short paragraph unless the user requests more.

For the top two roles daily, create at most one such artifact each. A proof artifact never includes proprietary data, an assertion about private company architecture, a fabricated result, or a multi-hour deliverable.
